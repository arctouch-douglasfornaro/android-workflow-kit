# Domain Pool

Source domains for structural transplant, organized by family. Each line names the domain and **the structural property that makes it transferable** — the shape you are actually borrowing, not the surface imagery.

Draw domains whose property matches your problem's structure sentence, not domains that sound topically related. **Mix across families** — a batch drawn entirely from biology all rhymes with itself.

---

## 🧬 Biological

- Immune system — distinguishes self from non-self, remembers past threats, mounts graded responses without a central controller.
- Ant colony — global routes emerge from agents following purely local pheromone rules; the trail self-strengthens with use and evaporates without it.
- Mycorrhizal network — fungi connect separate plants into a shared market, redistributing resources from surplus to deficit underground.
- Coral reef — a few keystone builders create physical structure that thousands of unrelated species then shelter and specialize inside.
- Murmuration — thousands of birds turn as one with no leader, each responding only to its seven nearest neighbors.
- Cell apoptosis — components are programmed to self-destruct on schedule so the whole stays healthy; death is a maintenance tool, not a failure.
- Slime mold — a single organism with no brain finds the shortest path through a maze by reinforcing what works and abandoning what doesn't.
- Symbiosis (lichen) — two unlike organisms fuse into one that survives where neither could alone; the partnership *is* the entity.

## 🚦 Operational

- Air-traffic control — many autonomous vehicles, one shared airspace, sequenced through a single congested channel with zero tolerance for collision.
- Restaurant kitchen brigade — a rush of unpredictable orders routed through specialized stations, synchronized so every plate on a table finishes at once.
- ER triage — demand always exceeds capacity, so incoming cases are ranked by urgency, not arrival order, and re-ranked continuously.
- Container shipping — wildly different cargo made interchangeable by one standard box, so the handling system never needs to know what's inside.
- Newspaper deadline desk — a hard, immovable cutoff forces a torrent of raw material to be filtered, ranked, and shipped every single day.
- Assembly line — a large task decomposed into fixed stations so unskilled hands produce expert output and the slowest station sets the pace.
- Warehouse pick-path — the bottleneck isn't the work but the *travel between* work; layout is optimized to shorten movement, not effort.

## 🎭 Social / Cultural

- Jazz ensemble — tight structure (the chord changes) plus radical local freedom (the solo); coordination without a script, built on "yes, and."
- Open-air market bazaar — no fixed prices; value is discovered live through negotiation, and the crowd's density is itself the signal of quality.
- Monastery rule — a written constitution governs a self-selecting community for centuries with almost no active enforcement; the rule does the managing.
- Jury deliberation — twelve strangers forced to convert disagreement into a single binding decision under a unanimity constraint.
- Potluck dinner — a full meal assembled with no coordinator; abundance and variety emerge from uncoordinated individual contributions.
- Guild masterwork — the right to practice is gated behind a produced proof of skill, judged by incumbents, not a credential or a fee.
- Barn raising — a task too big for any family is done in one day by the whole community, repaid not in money but in reciprocal future labor.

## ⚓ Historical / Trades

- Lighthouse network — a chain of independent stations, each doing one dumb reliable thing, that together make a whole coastline navigable.
- Guild apprenticeship — knowledge that can't be written down is transmitted by years of supervised doing, with graduated responsibility.
- Smuggling route — a system optimized to move value through hostile, monitored territory using redundancy, cutouts, and deniable handoffs.
- Cartography expedition — mapping unknown territory incrementally, where each trip's edge becomes the next trip's starting point.
- Field hospital — expert work performed under scarcity and chaos, where the constraint reshapes the whole practice around triage and improvisation.
- Relay post system — messages crossed empires fast because riders were fresh, not because any rider was heroic; the *handoff* was the design.
- Barn-door apprentice ladder — newcomers start on the safest tasks and earn access to riskier ones by demonstrated competence.

## ⚙️ Systems

- River delta — a single flow splits into many channels; sediment deposits where flow slows, and the channel map reshapes itself continuously.
- Power grid — supply and demand must match instant to instant across a shared network, with load-balancing and brownouts as pressure valves.
- Fermentation — a controlled, self-propagating transformation where you set initial conditions and then *let it run* rather than steering each step.
- Coral bleaching — a slow, invisible stress crosses a threshold and triggers sudden, hard-to-reverse collapse of a whole system at once.
- Forest fire succession — periodic destruction clears accumulated deadwood and is *required* for certain seeds to open; suppression makes the eventual fire worse.
- Beehive swarm decision — scouts advertise competing options, recruit proportionally to quality, and the colony commits once a quorum tips, with no vote counted.
- Watershed — thousands of tiny inputs aggregate downstream, so upstream events determine the character of everything below them.

---

## How to draw

For each batch (3-5 domains):

1. Write your problem's structure sentence first (actors, flows, bottleneck). Every domain you pick must rhyme with *that*, not with your industry.
2. Force spread across families. Take at least one **biological**, one **operational**, and one **social/cultural** so the transplants come from different underlying logics. A batch that's all systems-family will produce variations on one idea.
3. Prefer structural distance over vocabulary overlap. If a domain sounds topically close to your problem, that closeness is a warning, not a fit — the shared words usually hide the absence of a real structural match.
4. If a domain matches on two independent structural features (e.g. both "emergent from local rules" *and* "self-reinforcing"), promote it — dense rhymes transfer more.
